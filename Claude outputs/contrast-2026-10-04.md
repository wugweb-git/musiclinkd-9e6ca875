# Contrast, measured (brief §4), 4 Oct 2026

Measured on the production build (`.output`, served by wrangler) from the computed colours of the
live tokens, composited over their real grounds, with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula.
Grounds: page `--background` #ECECF1, card white, subtle (= card). AA needs 4.5:1 for normal
text, 3:1 for large text and for the visual boundary of a control (1.4.11).

| Secondary text on card / subtle | 16.41 | Pass |

| Hairline border (`--border` #D5D5DE) on page | 1.24 | **Fails 1.4.11 where the border is the only edge of a control** |

**Finding for the owner:** text inputs and outlined buttons are bordered with the 1px hairline,
which is 1.24:1 on the page. Most fields also sit on a white card (1.18:1 to the page) with a label
above, which WCAG accepts as identifying the field when the label and placement make it clear, but
outlined buttons and bare fields on the page rely on the hairline alone. Raising `--border` to a
3:1 grey (about #949499) changes the look of every card and field, so it waits for the owner's
decision; nothing was changed.
